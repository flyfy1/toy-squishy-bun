🍡 Squishy Bun: Squeeze a soft mochi bun. It slowly forgives you.

A glossy squishy bun with a tiny face. Press it, stretch it, squash it flat and watch it slowly rise back. Hold it hard and it gives up, dizzy. A tiny stress-relief toy, no sign-up.

How to play

  1. Press and hold the bun with a finger or the mouse. It flattens onto the desk and bulges out at the sides, with a soft dent where you press; two fingers press harder. Let go and it rises back slowly, like a slow-rise foam squishy.
  2. Drag from just outside its edge to stretch it. It pulls out like dough and snaps back with a wobble when you let go.
  3. Its face reacts: calm, then >_<, then dizzy spirals and a sweat drop. Hold a hard squeeze for about a second and it gives up with a squeaky wheeze and a line ("This is my job. I am fine."). Leave it alone and it dozes off; touch it and it wakes with a hop ("I was not asleep. I was proofing.").
  4. Use the bun button to give it a name and pick a skin: mochi, soup bun (小笼包 pleats), tangyuan or cat paw (猫爪捏捏). The name goes on a toothpick flag and on the sticky note, and both are added to the page link (?n=...&s=...), so a friend who opens it gets the same bun.

FAQ

Is there a squishy toy I can play with online?

Yes. Squishy Bun at squish.integ.life is a free virtual squishy. Press it with one or two fingers or drag its edge to stretch it; it bulges and slowly rises back like a slow-rise foam or mochi squishy. There is nothing to install and no account.

What happens if I squeeze too hard?

Hold a hard squeeze for about a second and the bun gives up: dizzy spiral eyes, a sweat drop, a squeaky wheeze and a deadpan line. It cannot be broken and there is no score to lose; let go and it slowly rises back.

Can I put a name on it?

Yes. Add a name and it appears on a small paper flag on a toothpick stuck in the bun, and on the sticky note beside it. The name is stored in the page link, so you can send a friend a bun named after your Monday.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It is built for touch, including two fingers at once, and on Android it gives small vibration ticks as you squeeze. It also works with a mouse.

Is Squishy Bun free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. The rubbery creak, the soft release, the wheeze and the wake-up boing are synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API, so there are no audio files to load. There is a sound on/off button, and on Android phones squeezing also gives small ticks of vibration; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.
